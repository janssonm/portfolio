The Impact of Color-Coded Sustainability Infographics on Short-term Memory

Oct. 2024 - Jan. 2025

A study on the impact of color relationships on short-term memory, focusing on sustainability-related information. Using the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and their color-coded infographics, my project group and I designed a memory game to evaluate how different color pairings influence recall. Through user testing and accuracy analysis, we explored whether harmonious or complementary colors enhance memory retention of sustainability concepts.

Tools & skills: collaboration, user research, UX design

A shared kitchen booking system - Media Technology and Interaction Design

Aug. 2024 - Oct. 2024

This design project explored the theme of interdependence by investigating everyday challenges faced by students living in shared corridor housing in Stockholm. Through user research, interviews, and observations, we identified gaps and pain points in how residents manage shared spaces. Building on these insights, we used creative ideation and iterative prototyping to develop a concept for an app that simplifies kitchen sharing by making time slot booking easier and addressing issues of space management and cleanliness.
